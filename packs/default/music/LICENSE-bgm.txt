BGM license and provenance record
=================================

Files: <song>-120.ogg, -132, -144, -156, -168 and -180.ogg for each song below:
       the song once through at that tempo, then three seconds in which it
       rings out. The six are the same bars, each a little harder than the
       one before; the game steps up and down this ladder one track per bar
       as the tallest stack grows and shrinks, and plays the songs one after
       another in random order (scripts/bgm_director.gd).
Arrangement and synthesis: made for this project ("Cold War Synth", 2026-10)

Songs
-----
korobeiniki-*.ogg  Korobeiniki. Russian folk song (19th century); public domain.
    Notes checked against the score of the English Wikipedia article.
kalinka-*.ogg  Kalinka. Ivan Larionov, 1860; public domain.
    Notes checked against John Chambers' ABC collection (trillian.mit.edu/~jc/music/abc/Russia/Kalinka.abc).
long-road-*.ogg  By the Long Road. Boris Fomin, 1924 (Dorogoi dlinnoyu); public domain.
    Notes checked against John Chambers' ABC collection (trillian.mit.edu/~jc/music/abc/Russia/DorogoyDalnoyu.abc).
god-save-the-tsar-*.ogg  God Save the Tsar!. Alexei Lvov, 1833; public domain.
    Notes checked against the score of the English Wikipedia article and The Everyday Song Book (1927) in John Chambers' ABC collection.
great-gate-*.ogg  The Great Gate of Kiev. Modest Mussorgsky, Pictures at an Exhibition, 1874; public domain.
    Notes checked against the score of the English Wikipedia article (the opening eight bars).
volga-boatmen-*.ogg  Song of the Volga Boatmen. Russian folk song, published by Mily Balakirev in 1866; public domain.
    Notes checked against an ABC transcription mirrored in John Chambers' collection (musicaviva.com, the refrain).
manchuria-*.ogg  On the Hills of Manchuria. Ilya Shatrov, 1906; public domain.
    Notes checked against John Chambers' ABC collection (trillian.mit.edu/~jc/music/abc/Russia/ManchurianHills.abc).

Only the melodies are taken from the songs. They were chosen from the
"Kaiserreich Russian State" playlist: the pieces whose composers' rights have
run out and whose notes could be checked against a published score.

Source
------
Rendered locally by tools/make_bgm.py (numpy + scipy synthesis, encoded with
ffmpeg libvorbis q3, 44.1 kHz stereo). No samples, recordings or third-party
audio are used: every sound is generated by the script.
